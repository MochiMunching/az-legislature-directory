# Outreach policy (set by SBDC leadership)

Until SBDC confirms its rules on legislative contact, drafts are educational only.

- Allowed: describing SBDC services, statewide and district results, client stories with permission, and the ESOP initiative.
- Not allowed until approved: asking the legislator to vote for or against a bill, to sponsor legislation, or to support a specific appropriation amount.
- Every draft is reviewed and approved by [SBDC TO PROVIDE: name or role] before anything is sent.

When SBDC approves requests, edit this file to say exactly what may be asked and by whom.
